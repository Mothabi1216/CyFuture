Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CYFUTURE's virtual internship bridge, candidate verification, institutional partnerships, and international placements.

What is CYFUTURE PTY LTD?

CYFUTURE PTY LTD is an international virtual internship bridge based in Maseru, Lesotho. We connect verified higher education students (undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate) with global host employers for 3-month remote placements.

How long does a virtual internship placement last?

All virtual internships on our platform operate on a structured 3-month placement cycle with regular milestone evaluations conducted by host employers and monitored by CYFUTURE Admin.

Who is eligible to apply as a student?

Students currently enrolled in or recently graduated from recognized higher education institutions in Lesotho and internationally across Undergraduate, Graduate, and Postgraduate levels are eligible to register.

What documents do students need to upload?

During or after registration, students must upload a clean copy of their National ID / Passport, an updated Professional CV, and official Proof of Academic Registration / Qualification for verification.

How does candidate matching work?

Once your documents are verified by CYFUTURE Admin and confirmed by your partner institution, our automated system matches your skills, field of study, and availability with international company opening requirements.

Why should colleges and universities partner with CYFUTURE?

Partnering institutions gain an administrative dashboard to verify student enrollments, track student career readiness, and offer students direct access to international practical workplace exposure without geographical boundaries.

How do institutions verify student records?

Designated institutional coordinators log into their dedicated portal to view pending student verification requests and confirm active enrollment status in just a few clicks.

How do international host companies submit internship openings?

Host companies register a portal account, submit company compliance documentation, and use the Employer Dashboard to define required technical skills, intern capacity, and preferred start dates.

Are interns evaluated during the 3-month program?

Yes. Host supervisors complete brief online progress evaluations through the company portal at Month 1, Month 2, and Month 3 to ensure quality work output and student development.
